What K is

K(species, environment,
   resource-regime, timescale) → n

A mapping, not a quantity. It takes a four-tuple and returns a single number n. Remove any one coordinate and K has no value at all — it is undefined, as a function is undefined outside its domain. K is therefore a property of a species–environment–resource–time tuple, never a property of a place.

The four arguments

species
Whose population is counted. Fixed here, because the question already fixes it.
environment
Which biosphere is assumed, in what condition.
resource-renewal regime
At what rate resources are replaced, and by what technology.
timescale
Over what horizon the other three are treated as constant.

Three readings, one symbol

K_bio
The ecological parameter of the logistic model. It presupposes a fixed resource regime, which is why that slider locks.
K_soc
The population sustainable given prevailing technology and trade. Here the resource regime is the variable, not a constant.
K_Earth
The collapsological usage: a bare constant, treated as fixed the way K_bio is. The fact button turns the bound statement into one.
